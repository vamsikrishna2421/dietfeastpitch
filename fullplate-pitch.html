FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much weight will I actually lose?

Most members lose 4–7 lbs in the first month, then 2–4 lbs/month for the next 3–4 months. Expect 18–25 lbs over 6 months at the Full Week plan, assuming you follow it and add 30 min of daily walking. Results vary with starting weight, activity, and consistency.

Will I be hungry?

Almost never. Volume eating is the whole point. Each meal contains 2–3x the food volume of a typical "diet" meal because the calories come from low-density, high-fiber, high-protein foods. Most members report being full all day.

How is this different from HelloFresh, Factor, or Sweetgreen?

HelloFresh makes you cook and isn't designed for weight loss. Factor is frozen and runs $13–17/meal. Sweetgreen is great but $14–18 per visit and you go to them. Diet Feast is the only service that delivers hot, fresh meals to your door twice a day, built specifically for weight loss, at $6–9 per meal.

What if I don't like an ingredient?

Onboarding form lets you exclude up to 5 ingredients (e.g., no beef, no celery, no shellfish, no mushrooms). You also pick vegetarian or standard each week.

Do I need to cook?

No. Every meal arrives hot and ready to eat. Open the container, eat the food. We deliver lunch at noon and dinner at 7 PM. The only thing in your kitchen you'll use is a fork.

What if I have a food allergy or medical condition?

List allergies during signup — we'll flag your account and never include those ingredients. For specific medical conditions (diabetes, kidney disease, etc.), please consult your physician before starting any weight-loss meal plan. We're a food service, not a medical service.

What if I'm not home at lunch or dinner time?

Meals come in insulated, food-safe containers that stay safe at room temperature for 3+ hours. Most members stash lunch in the office fridge or eat it when they get home. Delivery windows are ±30 minutes.

Greek yogurt (32 oz tub)

17g protein per 6 oz. Slow-digesting casein keeps you full. Calcium + probiotics. Doubles as marinade and salad dressing base.

Lentils (1 lb dry)

$2/lb = 8 servings = $0.25/meal of protein. Highest fiber:calorie ratio of any protein. Iron, folate, B vitamins, gut-feeding fiber.

Tofu (14 oz)

Variety lever. Plant complete protein, calcium-set. Absorbs sauces. One block = 3–4 servings at $0.75 each.

Whey protein

$0.60 per 24g protein serving. Fastest, cheapest, post-walk protein hit. Use 1–2 scoops/day max — supplement, not a meal.

Shirataki (3/wk)

10 kcal/pack. Glucomannan expands in stomach. Best for "noodle craving" nights. Cabbage covers the other 4 nights at 5% the cost.

Frozen vegetables

Cheaper than fresh, more nutritious (flash-frozen at peak), zero waste. Spinach, broccoli, mixed stir-fry — your freezer is your friend.

Cucumber & tomato

Hydration + crunch + minimal calories. Cucumber = 96% water = 16 kcal/100g. Eat freely as a snack.

Onion, garlic, ginger

Flavor base for everything. Antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, almost free per meal. The reason your food won't taste like diet food.

Lemons

Vitamin C, brightens flavor, lets you cut salt. Squeeze on everything. $0.50 per lemon, 8 meals worth of acid.

Daily precautions & what to watch for

Week 1 — adaptation: Expect mild headache, fatigue, possible constipation. Counter with 3 L water/day. Add a pinch of salt to broth and meals (your body sheds sodium on low-carb). This passes by day 7–10.
Cholesterol load: Max 3 whole eggs/day. Friday is your highest egg-yolk day — keep weekend yolks to 1–2. Get a lipid panel at month 3 to confirm response.
Gallstone risk: Rapid weight loss (>1.5 kg/wk sustained) raises gallstone formation. Your plan stays at ~1 kg/wk. Keep some fat in every meal (the olive oil and yolks).
Konjac timing: Take any prescription medications at least 1 hour before or 2 hours after shirataki — glucomannan can slow absorption.
Food safety: Cook chicken to 165°F internal (get a $10 thermometer). Refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours. Use cooked chicken within 3–4 days, eggs (cooked) within 5 days.
When to pause and call a doctor: Chest pain, severe right-upper-abdominal pain, persistent dizziness, constipation lasting >3 days despite water, or losing >1.5 kg/week sustained for 3+ weeks.

Movement layer (the multiplier)

10,000 steps/day

~400 extra kcal burn at your weight. Walking actually suppresses appetite. Worcester winter: try Auburn Mall or Greendale Mall walking before stores open.

2 strength sessions/week

15–20 min bodyweight or light dumbbells. Squats, push-ups, rows, lunges. Preserves muscle in deficit — the difference between fat loss and weight loss.

Skip intense HIIT for now

In aggressive deficit, hard cardio makes you ravenous and burns out. Save it for maintenance phase post-month 6.
